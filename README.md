## 📌 **Project Overview**
---

**Hekaya** is an interactive educational Islamic application designed for children and teenagers to explore and learn the stories of the Prophets in a simple, engaging, and enjoyable way.

The application transforms traditional storytelling into an interactive journey where users can explore the stories through short scenes, audio and text content, interactive activities, quizzes, and a journey map.

The goal is to help young users understand the key events, values, and lessons from each story while keeping the learning experience fun and engaging.

## 🎯 **Project Objectives**
---

- Present the stories of the Prophets in a simple and age-appropriate way.
- Make Islamic learning more interactive and engaging for children and teenagers.
- Help users understand the key events, values, and lessons from each story.
- Increase engagement through quizzes, activities, badges, and streaks.
- Allow users to track their learning progress.
- Provide parents with a simple way to follow their children's progress.
- Create a safe, reliable, and Ads-Free learning environment.

---

## 📦 **Project Scope**
---

The **Hekaya** application includes:

-  **Audio & Text Stories**
-  **Animated Stories**
-  **Interactive Journey Map**
-  **Interactive Prophets Tree**
-  **Key Events Highlighting**
-  **Values & Lessons**
-  **Interactive Activities**
-  **Quiz After Each Story**
